What each role can do

Staff
Answer guests in the Inbox, see bookings, add a walk-in booking, check guests in and out, set a room number and record payments.
Manager
Everything staff can do, plus change Settings and Facts, cancel bookings, block rooms, and read the Ratings and Enquiries reports.
Owner
Everything a manager can do, plus manage this team. A hotel always keeps at least one owner, and you cannot change your own role or remove yourself.
