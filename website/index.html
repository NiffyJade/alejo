Questions

Does it ever make things up?

No. It answers only from the facts you give it. A fact you have not filled in is withheld, and the question goes to your staff instead.

Which languages does it understand?

English as Nigerians speak it, Pidgin, and Yoruba-accented English on calls. A caller who speaks only Yoruba, or another language, is handed to your staff with their number.

Which channels work today?

Phone and Telegram. Guests who book by phone share their number with the bot once and the stay links up. WhatsApp and website chat are next.

What happens at 2 a.m. when it does not know?

The guest is told you are checking, and your staff group gets the question. Whoever replies first is heard: the reply goes to the guest, and if it starts with #kb it becomes an answer for next time.

How long does setup take?

Most of it is your facts: Wi-Fi, hours, prices, policies, bank details. We load 70 Nigerian-hotel answers, you fill the blanks, and we connect your phone number.

Where does guest data live?

In a private database set up for your hotel. A stay code is shown only to the chat linked to that booking, and only from the check-in date.
